About Medi Teck Engineering

Pakistan's leading provider of innovative medical engineering solutions, transforming healthcare infrastructure with cutting-edge technology and unmatched expertise.

Our Story

Medi Teck Engineering was founded by Habib Hussain with a vision to revolutionize healthcare infrastructure in Pakistan. Recognizing the critical need for world-class medical engineering solutions, he established the company to provide cutting-edge technology that meets international standards while being accessible to healthcare facilities across the country.

Under the visionary leadership of Habib Hussain, and with Muhammad Waqas leading technical operations, we have grown from our humble beginnings in Karachi to become a trusted partner for hospitals, clinics, and healthcare institutions nationwide. Our journey is driven by a commitment to excellence, innovation, and the belief that every patient deserves access to state-of-the-art medical facilities.

Today, we stand as pioneers in medical gas pipeline systems, oxygen generation plants, modular operation theaters, and comprehensive healthcare infrastructure solutions. Our team of expert engineers and technicians work tirelessly to deliver solutions that save lives and improve patient care.

What We Do

Comprehensive medical engineering solutions for healthcare facilities

Medical Gas Pipeline Systems

Design, installation, and maintenance of complete medical gas infrastructure including oxygen, nitrous oxide, medical air, and vacuum systems.

Oxygen Generation Plants

PSA-based oxygen generation systems providing continuous, cost-effective medical oxygen supply for hospitals and healthcare facilities.

Modular Operation Theaters

State-of-the-art modular OTs with HEPA filtration, laminar air flow, antibacterial panels, and integrated control systems.

Air Compressor & Vacuum Systems

Oil-free air compressors and central vacuum systems designed for medical applications with automatic controls and backup systems.

ICU & OT Ceiling Pendants

Single and double arm ceiling pendants with gas outlets, electrical sockets, and equipment mounting for ICUs and operation theaters.

Custom Medical Equipment

Design and manufacturing of customized stainless steel medical equipment, trolleys, and healthcare furniture tailored to your needs.
